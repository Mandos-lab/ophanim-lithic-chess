How it works

Use the box on the first screen. It understands three things:

A word (Greek or Hebrew). You get its number, built letter by letter like beads on a chain, then the other words with the same number, and where it appears in the Bible.

A number. You get every word with that number.

A verse, like Gen 1:1 or John 1:1-3. Also Yetzirah 1:1 (Sefer Yetzirah), LXX Gen 1:1 (the Septuagint, Greek numbering), Iliad 1:1, Odyssey 1:1, Theogony 1:116, Manetho 54:1 (fragment number : line), and Plato by page and section, like Cratylus 383a. You get each word with its number, and the total.

The Notebook tab remembers what you looked up, and the star button keeps a find in your private account. Tap any word in the results to look it up. To compare two words, use the Compare box under a word.

No keyboard for Greek or Hebrew? Tap "Greek letters" or "Hebrew letters" under the box.

Greek counts the 27 signs (stigma 6, koppa 90, sampi 900); final sigma is 200; accents are ignored.
