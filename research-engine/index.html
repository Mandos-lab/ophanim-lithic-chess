How it works

Use the box on the first screen. It understands three things:

A word (Greek or Hebrew). You get its number, built letter by letter like beads on a chain, then the other words with the same number, and where it appears in the Bible.

A number. You get every word with that number.

A verse, like Gen 1:1 or John 1:1-3. You get each word with its number, and the total.

Tap any word in the results to look it up. To compare two words, use the Compare box under a word.

No keyboard for Greek or Hebrew? Tap "Greek letters" or "Hebrew letters" under the box.

Greek counts the 27 signs (stigma 6, koppa 90, sampi 900); final sigma is 200; accents are ignored.
