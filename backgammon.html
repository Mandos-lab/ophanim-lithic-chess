    📜 ISOPSEPHIC METROLOGY & COSMIC HISTORY OF TAVLI

    The creator's reading of the board, from the Philosophy From A Stone framework. These are symbolic correspondences, not documented history of how the game was made. The counts and the Greek letter sums are real; the meanings are the reading.

    The 24 points (2Δ / 3Δ rays)

    12 above + 12 below = 24 vectors

    Read as the 24 hours of the solar day, the 24 runes of the Elder Futhark, and the 24 divisors of 360°: 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 9, 10, 12, 15, 18, 20, 24, 30, 36, 40, 45, 60, 72, 90, 120, 180 and 360. Each of those three counts is exactly 24.

    30 checkers (15 light, 15 dark)

    The synodic lunar month

    Fifteen stones of each colour make 30, read as a lunar month of 15 waxing and 15 waning days. The true synodic month is about 29.5 days, so 30 is its round number. Bearing off is the return: every stone runs the full circuit of the 24 points and leaves the board.

    12 dice faces

    6 + 6 = 12 · Tyche and Nomos

    Two dice carry 12 faces, read as the 12 zodiacal houses. The throw is Tyche (Fate), the rules are Nomos (Law). In Greek isopsephy Νόμος = 50+70+40+70+200 = 430, and Ἀριθμός (Arithmos, Number) = 1+100+10+9+40+70+200 = 430 as well.

    The central bar

    Hush 0.00 · Sampi axis · (0,0,0)

    The bar divides the inner and outer tables. In Portes and backgammon it is where a hit stone waits before it re-enters, so in this reading it is the zero-point valve: nothing is lost, everything returns through the centre. Sampi (Ϡ) is the old Greek numeral for 900.
