I · Before Words

The First Mirror

Before grammar there is a hum, a click, a sharp breath. A baby makes a sound with a tension in the face. The mother does not consult a book: she reads the face and the voice, and answers. In that answer the child learns it exists.

The thesis. Sound is not a label stuck on a world that was already there. It is the first engine of consciousness. The mother is the first decoder, and the loop of signal, reflection and identity is the First Mirror. Three Sumerian goddesses mark its stages: Namu, the sealed womb-sea (the M sound); Inana, the hungry kinetic ego; and Gestinana, who accepts the cycle and is satisfied.

The science. Mother and infant do mirror each other’s faces and voices. Researchers call this contingent responding, and it matters for early development. Paul Ekman’s Facial Action Coding System is real: Action Unit 26 is “jaw drop”.

The names. Nammu is the Sumerian primeval sea in the usual spelling; Inanna and Ereshkigal are standard. Reading the goddesses as stages of a voice is Carl’s own step. It is a reading, not an established fact about Sumerian religion.

II · The Alphabet as a Body

The 36-Pillar Palace

In 405 AD Mesrop Mashtots gave Armenia its alphabet. Carl reads its thirty-six letters as thirty-six pillars: a map of the mouth and throat, each sound a first-person act of the body.

The sounds. A: the jaw drops and the throat opens, “I am here.” M: the lips seal and the hum fills skull and chest, “I am within.” L: the tongue licks the sky, liquid between open A and solid G. G: a strike at the root of the tongue, gravity and ground. JJ: clenched molars, the friction of “I want” meeting “you must.” TS: a hissing jet through the teeth, the spark of an idea.

MEG, “one”: M (the womb) + E (existence emerging) + G (the ground). The whole journey from void to matter in one syllable.

The history. The Armenian alphabet is credited to Mesrop Mashtots, traditionally dated 405 or 406 AD. It began with 36 letters. Two more, Օ and Ֆ, were added in the Middle Ages, which is why modern counts say 38.

The meaning. Linguists treat the link between a letter and a sound as convention, though the shapes of the mouth are real and some sounds do carry feeling (the sound-symbolism literature). Reading each letter as a body state is Carl’s thesis, not a result of historical linguistics.

The word. Մեկ (mek) does mean “one”. The M + E + G reading is a way of hearing it, not an etymology.

Checked. The grid is right: 9 rows by 4 columns, and the values run 1–9, 10–90, 100–900, 1000–9000 (Wikipedia, Armenian numerals). Row five is the true middle.

Two corrections. First, the Armenian word for “is” is է, which is the letter for 7, not 5. The letter at 5, Ե, is a letter and not a word. Second, the tribute gives the Armenian word for God as 13,052. The modern spelling Աստված sums to 9,052, and the classical Աստուած sums to 13,652. Neither is 13,052, which looks like a slip for 13,652. Either way the word touches all four columns: Ա and Ծ from the first two, and Ս, Տ, Վ from the thousands. IV · Squaring the Circle

The 3-4-5 Bridge

The circle is heaven and the infinite. The square is earth and the cycle. Carl closes the gap with a right triangle.

The thesis. Three is the upright rod toward heaven. Four is the flat earth. Five, the diagonal, is the binder that weaves the two into one fabric. He hears the binder in the Armenian root KBR, and in Kabir turning to Kapor by the shift of B to P. In his “magpie reversal” the three becomes the womb-triangle, the four the bed inside it, and the five the child. Noah’s Ark and the founding of Yerevan are read as scaled hearths on this triangle.

Checked. 3² + 4² = 5². The Ark of Genesis 6:15 is 300 × 50 × 30 cubits, a ratio of 10 : 5/3 : 1, which is not a 3-4-5 shape. Yerevan grew from the fortress of Erebuni, founded in 782 BC by Argishti I (the date is on its foundation inscription).

The tetractys. The classical tetractys has four rows and ten points. The slides draw five rows and fifteen points, which is the next triangular number, not the Pythagorean figure.

V · The Mountain

Aragats & Ararat

For Carl the Armenian highland is a laboratory of fire and water, and the myths are what people saw.

Listen · 32 minutes

Aragats and the Four Pillars of Heaven

A recorded talk in English. It loads only when you press play.

The thesis. Mount Aragats is a giant colander, gats, straining boiling magma and venting gas. The dragon-slaying of Vahagn is a memory of a thermodynamic blowout, fire shot through the mountain into the sky. The Vishapakar, the dragon stones, were set around the volcanic slopes as markers of thermal lines. The classical name of the mountain he hears as “the atmosphere of the sun god’s cauldron.”

Checked. Aragats is a stratovolcano with four summits, 4,090 m north, 3,995 m west, 3,908 m east and 3,888 m south. It covers about a fifth of Armenia. Its last eruption is listed as unknown, and Holocene activity is only possible, so the “Plinian eruption around 3000 BC” in the manifesto is not established.

The dragon stones. They are real: carved stelae of 1.1 to 5.5 m, often near springs. A 2025 study dates them to about 4200–4000 BC and ties them to water, not to volcanic heat.

The name. The usual tradition links it to “Ara’s throne”, which sources call a folk etymology. Alagöz is Turkic for “variegated eye”. I could not find the claim that Thutmose III wrote “heaven rests on four pillars” of Armenia, so treat it as unconfirmed.

VI · The Name

Karekin, Sevan, Tatev

Karekin Sevan Tatev: three names, read as three stages.

Karekin. Kar, stone, and kin, woman: stone born of woman, the body as anchor. Sevan. The high black lake, a dark mirror turned up to heaven. Tatev. The monastery on the cliff, read as DA-TE-V: da, the giving and the stamp of the foot; te, the upright pillar, the backbone of Osiris; ve, the wing. Giving wings to the stone.

Checked. Tatev monastery stands above the Vorotan gorge in Syunik, and the 9th century is when it grew into a bishop’s seat. The common explanation of the name is Eustathius, a disciple of St Thaddeus. The “give me wings” reading (ta tev) is a folk etymology tied to a legend of a builder who fell, and it is the one Carl uses. Sev does mean “black”, and the old explanation of Sevan is “black monastery”. Kar is “stone” and kin is “woman”; whether the given name Karekin is built from them is not established.

VII · The Armenian–Greek Axis

Sulfide to Gold

A twelve-step deck follows the Armenian sounds across the Greek ones as an alchemy: from dark ore to gold, ending where the work began, on the letters ΦΥ ΨΗ.

The thesis. Armenian is the central crucible, the marriage bed, between the Western and Eastern sound systems. Armenian M and Mu are the container of the hum. The dialects pass through seven stages of rectification. The classical alphabet is a cage, and its 36 letters break into a wider grid. At the end the stone remembers how to fly, and the word for gold, ոսկի, closes the codex.

Checked. Մայր is “mother” and մուկ is “mouse”; the deck links them to Proto-Indo-European words for mother and mouse, which is the standard comparison. Ոսկի is “gold” (voski). The alphabet really went from 36 to 38 letters, and a 39th form, the ligature և, is counted in some lists. I have not audited the dialect table of stop consonants, and I could not confirm that Armenian was the source of Greek sounds, so read it as a model.

1/137. The fine-structure constant is about 1/137.036, not exactly 1/137. The slide’s 1 + 7 reading is numerology. The Armenian letter Ի is 20 and Է is 7, which is as the slide says.

VIII · Two Keys

Armenian & Greek

Carl’s Mother’s Code reads sound as body and myth. The Ellin Logos of Theologos Simaioforos reads the Greek letters as mathematics and light. The two meet in the figure of the human vessel: AR, the sun-engine, and MEN, the structuring mind.

The meeting. Seven Greek vowels, seven cervical vertebrae, seven colours, seven notes. Seventeen consonants for the spine below. The alphabet maps the body.

Checked. Greek has seven vowels (α ε η ι ο υ ω), and humans have seven neck vertebrae. Seventeen consonants plus seven vowels make twenty-four letters, and 12 thoracic + 5 lumbar vertebrae also make seventeen. That the one is a map of the other is the thesis; no source supports it as fact. The chapter on the spine is in The Reed and the Code. The Ellin Logos has its own page, still to come.

IX · The Board

Twenty Squares of Ur

The manifesto turns to the Royal Game of Ur as a map for the soul’s walk through a life.

The thesis. Seven pawns for the seven decrees (me) Inanna carried down. Four pyramid dice for four pillars. A central lane of friction. Five rosettes, granting safety and an extra throw, answer to five vowels. Ur itself is U, the abyss, and R, the sun-engine.

Checked. A side has seven pieces, the dice are four-sided, and the board has five rosettes (Royal Game of Ur page). Inanna takes seven me into the underworld in the myth; the match to the pawns is a reading. Greek has seven vowels, not five: the manifesto’s five (A E I O Y) leave out Η and Ω. The full game, with its own codex, is the Ur page.

To Carl & Sharron

A friendship of twenty-five years, with a teacher twenty-five years older. His search for the sources of speech set another soul on fire, and sent it off to the myths of Ararat and Olympus. Our own mouth is a loom, weaving the ten of earth with the nine of heaven.
