The Armenian Sphere
Letters, Numbers, Compass, Sound

Every Armenian letter is also a number. Type a word, add up its letters, and see where the total points on a 360 degree compass, on a turning sphere, and how it sounds.

The alphabet is credited to Mesrop Mashtots, about 405 AD, with 36 letters. Unlike the runes, Armenian letters really were used as numerals: Ա = 1 up to Ք = 9000, and this page uses those values. The two letters added later, Օ and Ֆ, have no place in the 36 and stand at the two poles of the sphere. Their values (10,000 and 20,000) are a house extension of this site.

I · The 360 Phonetic Calculator

Type a word, turn the sphere

Type an Armenian word and it is counted with the Armenian letter values. The Greek values stay for Greek words only. The total is also read as a bearing on the 360° compass: each 10° is one of the 36 letters. Inside the hologram, a jitterbugging core (cuboctahedron folding into icosahedron) sends one ray from the centre to each letter on the surface, and a gold path joins them in the order of the word. Drag the sphere to turn it, or tap a letter to add it.

Sound: each letter is a tone on a 432 Hz scale. Speech uses your device’s own voice; if it has no Armenian voice the word is read from its Latin spelling. The word list is a starter set, to be replaced by the full list.

II · How the Sphere Is Laid Out

Four bands, nine longitudes

The 36 letters sit in four rows of nine, the same grid as the Mother’s Code scroll. Each row is a latitude band: the ones (1 to 9), the tens (10 to 90), the hundreds (100 to 900) and the thousands (1000 to 9000). Each column is a longitude, 40 degrees apart. The pitch of a letter is 216 Hz times a step of the harmonic ratios (1, 9/8, 5/4, 4/3, 3/2, 5/3, 15/8, 2, 9/4) across the nine columns, and each row after the first raises it by a half octave.

Check. The 36 values add up to 49,995. By row: 45, 450, 4,500 and 45,000. The two later letters add 10,000 + 20,000 = 30,000, so with them the whole alphabet is 79,995.

III · Open for Audit

What is still a draft

This sphere was first built inside the Karaken page and now stands on its own, like the Greek, Hebrew and Futhark spheres. It is a working draft. These are the choices made so far, so each one can be changed:

  • The bearing. Total mod 360, with 360 shown instead of 0. The sector is the bearing divided by 10, one sector for each of the 36 letters.
  • The poles. Օ and Ֆ are shown at the south and north poles and are worth 10,000 and 20,000 by a house rule.
  • The word list is a starter set. The sounds are made in your browser; nothing is sampled and nothing leaves the page.
  • The Latin spellings used for speech when there is no Armenian voice are approximate.
