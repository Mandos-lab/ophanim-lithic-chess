Why can the Hebrew number here differ from the one under the Hebrew wheel? Both read the Greek by sound. The wheel reads the whole word and joins two-letter sounds (ΟΥ is one sound, “u”, so one ו). The compass reads letter by letter (Ο + Υ, two vowels, so two ו). For “Ο Θεός του Ισραήλ” that is one extra ו = 6: 1190 under the wheel, 1196 here. Neither is hidden or wrong; they are two reading methods.
