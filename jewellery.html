How It Will Work

Gold

18 kt is 75% gold, richer in colour and softer. 9 kt is 37.5% gold, harder wearing and lower in price. Either can be made for either pendant.

Custom

Size, chain length, stones and letters are agreed with the goldsmith before anything is cast. Prices and lead times are given on request.
