Salt and Spirit

When a collapsed body is wheeled into the emergency room, the first bag hung on the drip is not pure water. It is salt water.

That small fact says something large. Water alone cannot carry a current, and a cell sitting in it swells until it bursts. Add the right amount of salt and the same water becomes the voltage of a nerve, a heartbeat, a thought. This page starts in that room, then follows the salt into the crystal, into the Greek letters, and into the numbers that keep turning up beside it.

Pillar I · In the emergency room

The very first thing they hang on the drip

Pure distilled water is an insulator. It has almost no free charges, so it cannot carry an electric current, and a living cell cannot run on it.

Put a red blood cell in pure water and water rushes in through the membrane, because the inside is saltier than the outside. The cell swells and bursts. Put salt back in the right amount, 0.9 per cent, called normal saline, and the cell keeps its shape. That number is no accident: 0.9 grams in 100 millilitres is 154 millimoles of sodium and 154 of chloride per litre, about what the body fluid around your cells already holds.

A goldsmith knows this from the bench. Pure 24-carat gold is lovely and soft, and it will not hold a sharp shape. A little copper or silver in the mix, the limit, is what lets a ring keep its edge. Water is the pure gold here. Salt is the alloy that lets it hold a shape and carry a charge.

The Greek names for the two partners

The old Greeks had names for this pair. The apeiron (ἄπειρον) is the boundless, the formless fluid with no edge. The peras (πέρας) is the limit, the edge that gives a thing its shape. Water is the apeiron. The sodium and chloride ions of salt are the peras, little counted pebbles of limit: psephoi (ψῆφοι), the same word as the pebbles used for counting and voting. That is why the word for a pebble sits so close to the word for a number.

The battery in every cell

Every cell keeps a voltage across its membrane. A protein pump, the sodium–potassium pump, burns one ATP to push three sodium ions out and bring two potassium ions in. That uneven trade leaves the inside about 70 millivolts negative. The pump runs all the time and takes a large share of a nerve cell’s energy, which is why a brain is so hungry. Every nerve signal and every heartbeat is that stored voltage being let go for a moment.

Pillar II · Salt in the old stories

Salt on the table

Among the sayings handed down from Pythagoras is a plain one: set salt on the table. Writers such as Diogenes Laërtius pass it on as a reminder of justice. Salt keeps what it touches from rotting, and it is made by the sun working on the sea, which is exactly the kind of quiet fairness a table should have.

The same thought runs through the Gospel of Mark, in two neighbouring lines:

Mark 9:49   πᾶς γὰρ πυρὶ ἁλισθήσεται
“For everyone will be salted with fire.”

Mark 9:50   ἔχετε ἐν ἑαυτοῖς ἅλα, καὶ εἰρηνεύετε ἐν ἀλλήλοις
“Have salt in yourselves, and be at peace with one another.”

Notice where the salt goes. It is in yourselves (ἐν ἑαυτοῖς), and the peace is between you (ἐν ἀλλήλοις). The salt is inside each person first, which is just what the emergency room teaches: it is in every cell before it is anywhere else. Many manuscripts of verse 49 continue “and every sacrifice will be salted with salt” (καὶ πᾶσα θυσία ἁλὶ ἁλισθήσεται). Older Israel set salt on every offering as a sign of an unbreakable promise, the “covenant of salt” of Numbers 18:19.

The Greek for salt is ἅλς, and it adds up to 231 with the letter values (Α1 + Λ30 + Σ200). Keep that number in your pocket; it comes back in the next section.

Pillar III · The descent

From the eye, to the fire, to the salt

Dr. Theologos Simaioforos taught that Greek letters are not arbitrary shapes. Each one describes a force. Read that way, three words make a staircase from the unmoving to the solid.

Θ

Theta, the looking

Θ9 + Η8 + Τ300 + Α1 = 318

The old form of theta was a circle with a dot in the middle, the eye, the navel of the world (the omphalos). It is the still centre that only looks.

Θεῖον

Theion, the radiance

Θ9 + Ε5 + Ι10 + Ο70 + Ν50 = 144 = 12²

Theta looks, then the next letters set it moving: ε course, ι a steady stream of light, ο a bounded space, ν mind and law. The word means both “divine” and “brimstone”, the sulphur that burns. Its sum, 144, is 12 × 12.

🂝λς

Hals, the salt

Α1 + Λ30 + Σ200 = 231

Fire needs something to hold it. The salt is that holder: a hard, clear, cubic stone that the sea leaves behind when the sun has drunk the water. The staircase ends in a crystal.

The reading of each letter belongs to Simaioforos. The arithmetic belongs to everyone, and you can check every sum with the calculator below. By the way, the word for the Sun, ἥλιος, also adds to 318, the same as θῆτα, which is why the theta is called the eye of the sun. You will meet its famous cousin 666 further down, and see why the two are different numbers.

Pillar IV · The crystal

Why salt is a cube

Rock salt, or halite, grows and splits in cubes. Sodium and chloride sit on a three-way grid, each one surrounded by six of the other kind, one up, one down, one at each side. Join those six neighbours and you draw an octahedron, the cube’s dual. So inside every little cube there is an octahedron quietly working.

Count what the picture shows. A 3 × 3 × 3 block has 27 points. Take away the middle one and 26 are left on the outside, the hull. Now push the eight corners straight out along their diagonals by √3 − 1 ≈ 0.732, and you have eight spikes: 26 + 8 = 34. This is the same 26-point cage and 34-point star as in the Jitterbug page, seen here in a salt crystal.

The Madelung constant, a number that sums up how the charges of a salt crystal add together, comes out near 1.7476. It looks a lot like 1746. That is a curious echo, nothing more, but it is the kind of echo this page likes to listen for.

Pillar V · The letters

Soul and peace, letter by letter

In the Ellin Logos method of Dr. Simaioforos, a word is read by the sounds it is made of, one letter after another, each carrying a meaning. Here are two words most of us use daily.

ΨΥΧΗ = 1708

Ψ is the divine inner fire that comes from the Father. Υ is light gathered and concentrated. Χ is that fire placed in an earthly body. Η is its being revealed. So the soul is read as fire collected into a vessel and shown. The sum 1708 is also the phrase “the God of my heart”.

Ψ700 + Υ400 + Χ600 + Η8 = 1708
“ὁ θεὸς τῆς καρδίας μου” = 1708

ΕΙΡΗΝΗ = 181

Peace comes from eiro (εἴρω), which means both “I speak” and “I join together”. Ρ is the steady flow of light, Ν is the mind under law. So peace is read as light flowing into a mind that follows the law. Notice the Ν again: the same letter that closes ΘΕΙΟΝ and ΝΟΜΟΣ.

Ε5 + Ι10 + Ρ100 + Η8 + Ν50 + Η8 = 181

The letter meanings are the method’s reading, and you are welcome to test them against the sounds you speak. The sums are plain arithmetic.

Pillar VII · The two currents

Moon plus sun, one seed

John Michell, who spent his life on the measures of old sacred places, noticed two numbers standing across from each other that add to a third, one that Greek phrases keep returning to.

Moon1080

The Moon’s radius is about 1080 miles. The receptive, silvery, watery side.

Sun666

The numbers 1 to 36 add up to 666, the total of the Sun’s magic square of 6 × 6. The active, golden, fiery side.

Together1746

Both streams meet in one number: the seed of everything, from a grain of mustard to the “authority of the Lord”.

1080 + 666 = 1746

The same total is the value of these Greek phrases:

  • κόκκος σινάπεως (a grain of mustard seed) = 1746
  • ἐξουσία Κυρίου (the authority of the Lord) = 1746
  • τὸ κεκρυμμένον πνεῦμα (the hidden spirit) = 1746
  • ἡ δόξα τοῦ θεοῦ Ἰσραήλ (the glory of the God of Israel) = 1746

For a goldsmith this is an old friend: gold is the sun’s metal and silver the moon’s, and a good piece needs both. The salt of the earlier sections sits in the middle of this. Fire alone burns, water alone drowns, and salt is what lets them live together in one body.

A common mix-up, set straight

Helios is 318, the Sun’s number is 666

The word for the Sun and the number of the Sun are two different things, and both are right.

The word ΗΛΙΟΣ318

Η8 + Λ30 + Ι10 + Ο70 + Σ200 = 318

Add the letter values of ἥλιος and you get 318. Theta (ΘΗΤΑ) is also 318, which is why the two belong together: the eye and the sun it looks with.

The Sun’s square666

1 + 2 + 3 + … + 36 = 666

The old planetary squares give the Sun a 6 × 6 square, whose 36 cells hold the numbers 1 to 36. Add all 36 and the total is 666. So 666 is the Sun’s own number, not the spelling of its name.

Words that do spell 666 in Greek include:

  • Τειταν (Teitan, the “Titan” that the early writer Irenaeus suggested for the beast of Revelation)
  • ὁ Σεράπις (ho Serapis, the sun-god of Alexandria)
  • Συήνη (Syene, today’s Aswan)

A small bonus: 318 is also very close to 1000 ÷ π = 318.31, and it is the number of trained men that Abram took to rescue Lot in Genesis 14:14. Numbers like these are why theta feels like a number of the Sun. Keep the Sun’s 666 and its 318 each in its own place, and the arithmetic stays clean.

Pillar VIII · Old chemistry

Sulphur, mercury and salt

Alchemists spoke of three principles: sulphur for the burning, mercury for the flowing, and salt for the fixing. In the nineteenth century Éliphas Lévi (born Alphonse Louis Constant, 1810–1875) gathered them under the name astral light, a fluid with a positive side (sulphur) and a negative side (mercury). When they come into balance, he said, they settle into salt, and from balance comes stability.

Lévi linked the cubic stone of the alchemists to the “rock” in the saying to Peter, Petros and petra (Πέτρα = 486, and the Aramaic form Κηφᾶς = 729 = 27²). Later alchemical pictures, among them those attributed to Basil Valentine, draw salt as a cube.

Lévi also gave a warning that fits a page about voltage. The astral light, he said, is a blind current. Unattended, it only stirs up old fears and appetites, and he wrote that in London in 1854 his own attempt to call up the spirit of Apollonius of Tyana left his arm numb. His answer was balance and a steady will. A cube that holds everything closed is safe but cold, which is why the next part asks what happens when the cube opens.

Pillar IX · Opening the cube

From the cage of 26 to the star of 34

Go back to the crystal and its 26 outside points. That is the cage, the closed salt box. Push the eight corners out along their diagonals and the box grows eight spikes, 34 points in all, like a lamp throwing light instead of holding it. The Jitterbug shows the same step in motion.

26 + 8 = 34

Notice how the alphabet ends. The 27th sign is sampi (Ϡ = 900), a sign that sits at the end of the row and, in the classical alphabet, has fallen silent. The word for silence, σιγή (ΣΙΓΗ = 221), is a good name for that stillness after the last letter. Salt gave us the limit. Silence is what is left when the limit has done its work and the signal goes out clean.

There is one more line that belongs here. Ignorance, ἄγνοια, adds to 135, and 135 is 27 × 5, the number we met in the counting. Not-knowing and the whole alphabet share the same measure. Keep that in mind when you next say you do not know something: you are standing exactly where the alphabet stands.
