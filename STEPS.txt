ENGINE 178 - Liddell and Scott lexicon

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open the folder "research-engine" inside it. Copy the 2 files (index.html and lsj.enc) into your repo's research-engine folder. Say Replace if asked.
3. GitHub Desktop: write "lexicon" in the Summary box, press Commit to main, then Push origin.
4. Wait about 1 minute. Open the engine, press Ctrl+F5, sign in.
5. Read tab: new box "Lexicon: Liddell and Scott". Type a Greek word (accents optional), press Look up.
