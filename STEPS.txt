ENGINE 179 - explanations on every box, plus the Liddell and Scott lexicon (this replaces 178)

1. Unzip this folder.
2. Open the folder "research-engine" inside it. Copy the 2 files (index.html and lsj.enc) into your repo's research-engine folder. Say Replace if asked.

5. Type a word on Explore. Each box now has a short green-edged note saying what it is. "Hide notes" under the search box turns them off.
