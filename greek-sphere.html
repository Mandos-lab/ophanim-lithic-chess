The Greek Sphere
Letters, Numbers, Compass, Sound

Every Greek letter is also a number. Type a word, add up its letters, and see where it points on a 360 degree compass and how it sounds.

Greek writes its numbers with its letters, so every word has a value. This page uses 27 letters: the 24 classical ones plus ϛ stigma (6), ϟ koppa (90) and ϡ sampi (900). They fall into three bands of nine: units (1 to 9), tens (10 to 90) and hundreds (100 to 900).

I · The 360 Phonetic Calculator

Type a word, turn the sphere

Each letter is a node on the sphere. The total of the letters becomes a bearing on the gold compass (the total divided by 360, keeping the remainder), and the bearing falls in one of 27 sectors, one for each letter. Every letter also has a pitch on the 432 Hz scale. Drag the sphere to turn it, or tap a node to add its letter.

How to find a word in the Septuagint

  1. Type the word in the dictionary box, for example ουρανός, and press Show definition here for its meaning. The Logeion option gives the full Liddell–Scott entry.
  2. Open a book above, such as Genesis, then use your browser’s find (Ctrl+F) on that chapter. Genesis 1:1 has the word.
  3. Search the stem without accents, here ουραν. The text uses several endings (οὐρανός, οὐρανοῦ, οὐρανόν, οὐρανῷ), and the stem finds them all.
  4. To see which Hebrew word it translates, open the same chapter on the Hebrew page. Here οὐρανός stands for שמים (shamayim).
  5. For the New Testament, use the Greek New Testament box above in the same way. Press Speak the word beside the dictionary to hear the word, and Repeat (slowly) to hear it again.

Notice. These links open other websites in a new tab. This site does not host or copy these texts, and each site has its own terms. Editions differ from one another, so check which one you are reading before comparing a word or a total.

IV · How the Sphere Is Laid Out

Three bands, nine longitudes

Row by row: the letter, its value, and its pitch. The pitch is 216 Hz times a step of the harmonic ratios (1, 9/8, 5/4, 4/3, 3/2, 5/3, 15/8, 2, 9/4) across the nine columns, and each band up raises it by a half octave. The compass sectors run in this same order, a letter every 13.3 degrees.

Check. The 27 values add up to 4,995. The 24 classical letters add up to 3,999, and the three archaic ones (6 + 90 + 900 = 996) make the rest.

V · Open for Audit

What is still a draft

This is the first working version. The mathematics and the topology of the sphere come next, and we will audit and configure them together. These are the choices made so far, so each one can be changed:

  • The sphere. 27 nodes, three latitude bands by nine longitudes of 40 degrees. The Armenian sphere has 36 nodes in four bands; this layout follows the three-by-nine order of the number-letters.
  • The bearing. Total mod 360, with 360 shown instead of 0. The sector is the bearing divided by 360/27 = 13.33 degrees.
  • The pitches. The 432 Hz scale as described above. Nothing is sampled: the tones are made in your browser.
  • Letters. Stigma, koppa and sampi are included, so the alphabet is a clean 3 x 9 grid and ends on sampi (900). Accents and breathings are ignored. A final ς counts as sigma.
  • Word values. Plain isopsephy: add the letter values. Nothing is added for the diphthongs: ΑΙ is Alpha plus Iota.
