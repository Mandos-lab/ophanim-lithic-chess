About

Philosophy From A Stone is a project by Mandos Polycarpou (@Mandos16), a former goldsmith who studies shape, sound and meaning. It brings together the Ophanim Field Console, the Lithic game boards, Ezekiel's Reed//deck and the library.

The guiding idea is in the two lines at the top of every page: listen for the shape in a word, and be responsible about what you bring into the world with it.
