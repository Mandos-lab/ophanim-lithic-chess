What is arithmetic and what is reading

Every sum on this page is correct and can be repeated with a pencil. The links between them, such as the net and the Lord God, or Christ and 37 × 64, are the reading, and the reading is where the argument lives. One caution: these totals count ου as two letters, ο 70 and υ 400, as ordinary isopsephy does. If a phonetic rule counts it as one sound, the totals change, so say which rule is in use.
