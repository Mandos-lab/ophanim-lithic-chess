Free member accounts

Joining is optional. Visitors can look words up and explore every page without an account. A free member account lets you save words and keep them on your own dashboard, on any device.

What is stored when you join:

  • Your email address, used only to sign you in, to send the confirmation link and to send a password-reset link if you ask for one.
  • Your password, which is kept in scrambled (hashed) form by the sign-in service. I cannot see it or read it back.
  • Your display name, if you choose one. It does not have to be your real name.
  • The words you save in the “My words” panel (the English word and the forms you add), and the time you joined.

I do not sell this information and I do not use it for advertising. The database and the sign-in service are run by Supabase, which acts on my behalf as a processor. The project is hosted in the EU (Ireland). Supabase also sees your IP address when your browser contacts it.

Download and delete. On your dashboard you can download your words at any time. “Delete my account” removes your sign-in, your name and every word you saved, for good. It cannot be undone.

Guests and online games

If you play an online game as a guest, the site creates an anonymous guest session with a random identifier. There is no email or password. While you play, your display name, the game moves and any short chat lines you send in that room are stored so that the other player can see them. Guest games and chat lines are not removed automatically yet; I clear old rooms by hand. Members who delete their account have the chat lines they wrote, and the rooms they hosted, removed at the same time.

Content from other websites

Some pages can show content that comes straight from another company. When it loads, that company receives your IP address and your browser details and may set its own cookies. These stay off until you choose "Allow third-party content" in the cookie box.

  • YouTube and Vimeo videos (radio slots, the media panel, the Library).
  • X (Twitter) timeline on the Field Console.
  • Any other site you open inside the Field Console or Library frames.

Other connections your browser makes

These are needed to show the page itself, so they are not switched off by the cookie box. They do not set cookies, but the provider sees your IP address.

  • Google Fonts loads the typefaces on the game and codex pages.
  • jsDelivr (a content network) delivers the sign-in library. It loads only when you open the join or sign-in form, or play online, or if you are already signed in.
  • CoinGecko supplies the crypto price ticker, and a small function on this site fetches stock-index prices from Stooq.
  • Vercel hosts the site and keeps ordinary server logs (IP address, page, time) for security and running the service.

Sound and read-aloud

The game sounds and the optional drone are made inside your browser. No sound files are downloaded from other websites. The "Read aloud" button uses your device’s own speech voices, and the page prefers voices that run on your device. Some browsers also offer online voices; if your browser chooses one of those, the text being read may be sent to the browser’s maker. Recordings of articles, where they exist, are played from this site.

Your rights

If you are in the EU or UK you have the right to ask what data is held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to object to its use, and to complain to your local data protection authority (in Cyprus, the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection). Member accounts are described above, and you can delete yours yourself from the dashboard. For anything else, or if you cannot sign in, write to the contact address above. Beyond the member and guest data described here and the hosting logs, nothing about you is stored on my side.
